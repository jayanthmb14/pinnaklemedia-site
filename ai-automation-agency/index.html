What is an AI automation agency?

An AI automation agency builds software workflows that use AI to do repeat business tasks for you, such as finding leads, writing first drafts of emails, sorting replies and updating your CRM. The goal is fewer manual steps and faster follow-up, with a person still checking the important parts.

Is cold email still worth it?

Yes, if it's done carefully. Cold email works when the list is small and right, the message is short and personal, and the sending domains are set up and warmed properly. It fails when people blast thousands of generic emails. We keep volumes low, set up separate sending domains and warm them up, and follow the anti-spam rules in each market you sell to. It's how Pinnakle Media found its own first clients.

Who is it for?

  • Coaches and consultants who get leads from content but lose them in DMs and spreadsheets.
  • Founders who want a steady flow of sales calls without hiring a sales team yet.
  • Service businesses and agencies that repeat the same onboarding and reporting every month.

Who is it not for?

  • Anyone who wants to send thousands of untargeted emails a day. We don't do that.
  • Businesses that don't have a clear offer yet. Automation speeds up what works; it can't fix an offer nobody wants.
  • Teams that want a fully custom app. We build on proven tools, not from scratch.

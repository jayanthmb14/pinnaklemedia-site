The LinkedIn playbook

Ghostwriting that turns a profile into a pipeline.

Run since November 2024 with our LinkedIn specialist Zaid for coaches, founders and an energy-industry leader. Brand first, pitch later.

  1. Profile & bannerHeadline, about section and a custom banner before a single post goes out.
  2. 30+ posts a monthA week of posts drafted ahead in a shared doc for approval, then scheduled. Underperformers get pulled.
  3. 60–70 comments a dayThe 33/33/33 rule: a third on small creators, a third on peers, a third on top voices within minutes of posting.
  4. 10 DMs a dayOutbound from a prepared lead sheet only after a month of trust-building; lead-magnet posts with “comment to get”.

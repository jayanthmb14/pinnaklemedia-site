Before you ask

The questions we get on the first call.

Promo films, back-catalogue re-edits, reel-cover systems, carousels, the sample edit, how work is tracked, and what a dollar gets you. Plain answers, in order of how often they come up.

What does Pinnakle Media do?

We run the content for creators, coaches, podcasters and the companies behind them. That means editing (Shorts, Reels, TikTok, long-form YouTube, podcasts), thumbnails and carousels, LinkedIn ghostwriting, posting to seven platforms, a weekly report, and the lead follow-up system we call PinnakleFlow. You record. We run the channel.

Is Pinnakle Media a video editing agency, a personal branding agency or a social media marketing agency?

All three, depending on what you need. Most clients start with editing. Within a few months the same team is writing hooks, designing covers, posting daily, replying to comments and reporting every Friday, which is what a personal branding or social media agency does. We prefer to be judged on the case studies on this page rather than the label.

Who is it for?

Creators from about 40K subscribers up to a million, coaches and course creators with an offer to sell, podcasters, real estate agents, finance educators, clinics, and B2B founders who want a LinkedIn presence. Most clients are in the US, Canada and the UK, then Australia, Europe and the Gulf, and we work with established brands wherever they are. We work from Bengaluru, so edits land overnight.

How fast is the turnaround?

Shorts within 24 hours of the raw footage, long-form within 48, revisions within 12. A new client has a content calendar approved by day five and posts going out the same week.

What is a promo film and a testimonial film?

A promo film is a 30 to 90 second piece that sells one thing: a launch, a cohort, a course, an event. A testimonial film is a client of yours telling their story on camera, cut so the result comes first. We script both, direct you remotely if needed, edit, and deliver versions for every platform. Both are included in most retainers and can be ordered on their own.

What is a back-catalogue re-edit?

Your old videos already have views and search history. We go through the back catalogue, pick the ones that still answer a question people search for, and re-cut them with a new hook, new captions, a new thumbnail and a description built to rank. For one client that doubled views on a two-year-old video. It is the cheapest growth most channels never do.

What is a reel-cover system?

A set of cover templates for Reels and Shorts so your grid reads as one brand before anyone presses play. Each cover carries the hook text, your colours and your face or logo in the same place every time. We build the system once in your brand kit, then every editor uses it, so new covers take minutes and never drift.

How do carousels work?

A carousel is a swipeable set of slides on Instagram or LinkedIn. We write them from your videos, your posts or a brief, design them in your brand kit, and deliver the slides with the caption, keywords and a pinned comment. For coaches they are usually the best-saved format we post. We built an internal engine that turns a brief into a finished, numbered carousel pack, so volume is not a problem.

Do you do a free sample edit?

Yes. Send one raw video and we cut a Short from it before you commit. You see the hook, the captions and the cover in your brand, and you decide.

How does the work get tracked?

In Pinnakle OS, the app we built to run the studio. Every piece of content is a card with a status (raw received, editing, QC, your review, approved, posted), an owner and a deadline. You get a portal with the board, review links, the posting calendar and the reports. Nothing lives in a WhatsApp thread.

What is PinnakleFlow?

The follow-up system for the leads your content creates. Every DM, comment, call and form fill is answered within sixty seconds, in your words, booked on your calendar, moved along a pipeline and followed by a review request. It runs on your own number and accounts. You can start it for a dollar for seven days.

Do you use AI, and will my content look like it?

We use AI where it saves you a camera day: trained avatars for Shorts when you cannot record, cloned voice-over for documentaries, generated B-roll, and tools that help us write hooks faster. Every cut is still edited and checked by a person, and anything AI-made is shown to you first. Nothing goes out that looks generic.

How much does it cost?

Retainers depend on the platforms and the weekly output. We go through it on a 15-minute call and send a one-page scope the same day. There are no prices on this page because the scope is different for a podcaster and a clinic. Month to month, no lock-in.

How do I start?

Book the 15-minute call. You leave with a drawn map of your content path and the three things that would move the numbers first. If it fits, the onboarding kit goes out the same day and your calendar is approved by day five.

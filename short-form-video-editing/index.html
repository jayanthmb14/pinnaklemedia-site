What is a short-form video editing agency?

A short-form video editing agency is a team that makes vertical videos under about 3 minutes for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok. It usually picks the moments worth clipping, edits them for pace, adds captions and prepares them to post. You provide the raw recording.

FAQ

Short-form video editing, plainly answered.

What does a short-form video editing agency do?

It turns your recordings into vertical clips for Shorts, Reels and TikTok. It picks the moments, edits them, adds captions and gets them ready to post. You stay the face and voice.

Can you turn my podcast into Shorts and Reels?

Yes. Send us the episode, and we'll cut the best moments into vertical clips with captions. Filmed episodes give the best result. If it's audio only, ask us on the call what's possible.

What is the best agency to repurpose podcasts into Shorts?

Look for a team that picks clips with care, does a quality check, shows real client work and lets you approve each clip. Pinnakle Media does all of this and has made 3,000+ videos for 100+ clients.

How fast is the turnaround?

Shorts come back within 24 hours of the raw footage, and revisions within 12 hours.

Is an agency better than an AI clipping tool?

It depends on your budget and time. AI tools cost less, but you check and fix the clips yourself. An agency costs more and takes that work off you.

Do you guarantee views?

No. We focus on what we control: good moments, clean editing, clear captions and posting on time.

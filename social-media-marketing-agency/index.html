What does a social media marketing agency do?

A social media marketing agency plans, creates and publishes content on social platforms for a business, then tracks what works. A good one turns your expertise into posts people want to watch, keeps you posting on a steady schedule, and tells you which posts bring leads. You keep running the business.

How it works

What does Pinnakle Media do for your social media?

  1. Plan.

    A short call to pick your 3–4 content topics, the platforms and your posting rhythm.

  2. Capture once.

    You record one video or talk with us on a call each week.

  3. Create.

    We edit Shorts, Reels and TikToks, write LinkedIn posts and captions, and design thumbnails and carousels.

  4. Approve.

    A second person checks every piece. You approve or leave notes before anything is posted.

  5. Post and report.

    We post across your channels and send a report every Friday, then adjust the next batch.

Why video-first for coaches and small businesses?

Short video is how most people find a coach or a local expert today. One good clip on Reels or Shorts can reach people who'd never see a text post. Video also builds trust faster, because people hear how you talk before they book a call. That's why every Pinnakle Media social plan starts with video and builds the written posts around it.

What is LinkedIn personal branding?

LinkedIn personal branding is the work of making a person known on LinkedIn for a clear topic, so the right buyers, partners or hires trust them before they meet. It covers your profile, the posts you publish, how you comment and how you show proof of your work.

How it works

What does a LinkedIn personal branding agency do?

A LinkedIn personal branding agency plans, writes and publishes LinkedIn content for you, so you build an audience without spending hours writing. At Pinnakle Media that means:

  1. Positioning call.

    We agree on 2–3 topics you want to be known for and who you want to reach.

  2. Profile fix.

    Headline, About section, featured posts and banner that say what you do and who for, before a single post goes out.

  3. Voice interviews.

    Short recorded calls each week. We turn your words into posts.

  4. Writing and design.

    Text posts, carousels and short video clips. 30+ posts a month, drafted a week ahead in a shared doc for your approval.

  5. Posting, engagement and reporting.

    We post on schedule, handle daily comments and DMs, and send a report every Friday.

FAQ

LinkedIn personal branding, plainly answered.

What is the best LinkedIn personal branding agency for founders?

Look for an agency that writes from interviews with you, shows real client examples, and lets you approve every post. Pinnakle Media does all three and can also turn your videos into LinkedIn clips.

What is a LinkedIn ghostwriting agency?

A LinkedIn ghostwriting agency writes posts that are published under your name. The ideas come from you; the agency does the writing, editing and posting.

Will the posts sound like me?

Yes. We write from recorded calls in your own words, and you approve every post before it's published.

How long does LinkedIn personal branding take to work?

Most people see steady reach after 2–3 months of regular posting. Inbound leads usually take longer and depend on your offer.

What is a YouTube video editing agency?

A YouTube video editing agency is a team that turns your raw footage into finished videos for your channel. It handles cuts, captions, graphics, thumbnails and often posting. You get a team with a QC step, so the work keeps going when one editor is away. With a solo freelancer, it stops.

How it works

What does Pinnakle Media do for your channel?

We run the full loop, from raw footage to a posted video:

  1. Audit and onboard.

    We study your channel and your three best videos.

  2. Edit, caption, design.

    Cuts built for retention, captions, thumbnails, motion graphics, intros and B-roll, in your brand style. We use AI avatars and voice only where they help.

  3. QC, then Frame.io.

    A second person checks every edit before you see it. You leave notes in Frame.io.

  4. Post everywhere.

    We publish to up to 7 platforms.

  5. Measure, report, repeat.

    We look at what worked and adjust the next batch.

What does a personal branding agency do?

A personal branding agency is a team that builds a person's public reputation through content. It plans what you'll talk about, turns your ideas into posts and videos, publishes them on the right platforms and tracks what works. The goal is to make you the known name in your niche, so leads and opportunities come to you.

How it works

What does Pinnakle Media do for your personal brand?

  1. Find your angle.

    A short call to pick 3–4 topics you can own and the platforms that fit (usually LinkedIn plus YouTube or Shorts).

  2. Capture once.

    You record one video or talk with us on a call. That one session becomes the raw material for the week.

  3. Write and edit.

    LinkedIn posts in your voice, long-form YouTube edits, Shorts and Reels, podcast clips and thumbnails.

  4. QC and approve.

    A second person checks everything. You approve or leave notes before anything goes out.

  5. Post and report.

    We publish, track what worked and adjust the next batch.
